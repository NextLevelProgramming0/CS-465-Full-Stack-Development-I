Experience Kayaking!

April 03, 2023
by: Juan De La Cruz

Kayaking is a peaceful way to explore the shoreline while enjoying fresh air, clear water, and views that cannot be reached from land. Guests can choose a relaxing guided paddle or venture out independently in designated areas.

Each excursion begins with a short safety briefing and an equipment check. Life jackets and paddles are provided, and our guides explain the best route for the day's weather and water conditions.

Reservations are recommended for morning trips. Bring sunscreen, water, secure footwear, and a waterproof case for personal items.
