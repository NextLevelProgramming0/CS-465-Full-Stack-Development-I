Travel

  • Gale Reef

    Gale Reef offers calm water, excellent visibility, and a colorful coral landscape that is welcoming to new and experienced divers.

    Guided excursions include safety equipment, local instruction, and plenty of time to explore the reef's tropical fish and natural formations.

  • Dawson's Reef

    Dawson's Reef is known for dramatic underwater scenery, schools of tropical fish, and deeper routes that reward confident divers.

    Morning trips offer the clearest conditions, while experienced guides help visitors choose a route suited to their comfort and ability.

  • Claire's Reef

    Claire's Reef features gentle currents, bright coral gardens, and shallow viewing areas that are ideal for snorkeling and relaxed dives.

    Visitors can enjoy a guided half-day trip with equipment, refreshments, and helpful instruction provided by the resort team.
