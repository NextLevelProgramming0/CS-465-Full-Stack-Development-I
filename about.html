About

Welcome to Travlr Getaways

Travlr Getaways brings comfortable accommodations, fresh meals, and memorable coastal activities together in one relaxing destination. Our team helps guests make the most of every day, whether they prefer a quiet beach visit or an active island adventure.

We are committed to friendly service, clean and inviting rooms, and carefully planned experiences. From booking through departure, our goal is to make every visit simple, comfortable, and enjoyable.

Our Crew

Our experienced hospitality and activity teams are available to answer questions, recommend local experiences, and help guests throughout their stay.

Amenities

Guests enjoy comfortable rooms, on-site dining, beach access, guided water activities, equipment rentals, and welcoming shared spaces.
